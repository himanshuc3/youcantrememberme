# Privacy Policy: Catch Me Up (DRAFT)

Catch Me Up answers questions about TV shows. It does not require an account and does not
collect, store, or share personal information. Requests (show name and episode range) are
processed to produce a recap and are not linked to you.

Episode information comes from TVmaze (https://www.tvmaze.com, CC BY-SA 4.0).
